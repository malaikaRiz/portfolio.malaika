About

I’m a graphic designer who also studies Computer Science.

Design is where I get to be expressive: choosing a typeface, balancing a layout, and finding the right colors for a message. My computer science background gives me a logical, detail-minded way of working, so the things I make are both considered and clear.

I work on carousels, brand identities, social media posts, and stories, always aiming for visuals that feel intentional.
